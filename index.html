Contingency table — "Prefer online shopping?" by age (n = 300)
A contingency (cross-tab) table counts how two categorical variables occur together. Each inner cell is a joint count; the final row and column are the margins (totals).

Reading down the % column, preference falls sharply with age (80% → 55% → 30%) — the two variables look associated. A χ² test (see Hypothesis Testing) checks whether that pattern is beyond chance.
